Market Insight

Why Invest in Arkansas Land

01

Affordable Entry Point

Arkansas remains one of the most affordable land markets in the country, letting buyers acquire meaningful acreage without the price tag of coastal or Sun Belt markets.

02

Landowner-Friendly Taxes

Property taxes here rank among the lowest in the U.S. — roughly 0.62% of assessed value — and Arkansas levies no state inheritance tax, making land easier to hold and pass down.

03

Land That Actually Produces

From Ozark and Ouachita timberland to Delta farmland, Arkansas offers real, usable acreage — not just speculative dirt — for recreation, agriculture, or future development.

04

Growth With Staying Power

Corporate expansion and steady population growth in Northwest and Central Arkansas are driving durable, long-term demand for land — not a short-lived spike.
